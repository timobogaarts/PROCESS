# Next steps — archive of sections 1–25 (and later dated state entries)

This was a verbatim archive of dated "state" snapshots moved out of `next_steps.md` as it
grew, each numbered section a point-in-time record of what had just landed and what was
open. All of it describes work that is now done; the port's current status lives in
`next_steps.md` and `unit_registry.md`. Numbered sections are still cited by number from
various code comments and other audit records — those citations are unchanged, they just
now resolve into git history rather than into this file: `git log -- functional_process/
_audit/next_steps_archive.md` and `git show <commit>:functional_process/_audit/
next_steps_archive.md`. The handful of measured constants and refutations from this
archive worth keeping live in `_audit/tried_and_rejected.md`.
